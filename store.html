What is in the book

  • 36 chapters in five units, following a standard first-year university physics sequence.
  • Each chapter has a learning objective, short sections with worked examples, a list of common mistakes, a review of key terms and equations, and practice questions at three levels: conceptual questions, problems and challenge problems.
  • Every chapter is also labelled with one problem-solving skill, such as mathematical modelling or checking limiting cases, and the Index by Skill at the back lists the chapters under each skill.
  • Answers to all practice questions, a formula sheet, a glossary, and a short maths toolkit.
  • Chapters line up with the free OpenStax University Physics textbook, so you can read the two side by side.

Contents

Unit 1: Mechanics

How bodies move, why they move, and what is conserved while they do.

  • Motion Along a Straight Line and Vectors
  • Motion in Two and Three Dimensions
  • Newton's Laws of Motion and Applications
  • Work, Kinetic Energy and Potential Energy
  • Linear Momentum and Collisions
  • Fixed-Axis Rotation and Angular Momentum
  • Fluid Mechanics

Unit 2: Waves and Acoustics

Repeating motion and the disturbances that carry it through space.

  • Oscillations and Travelling Waves
  • Sound and Wave Interference

Unit 3: Thermodynamics

How huge numbers of particles produce temperature, pressure and engines.

  • Temperature, Kinetic Theory and Gas Laws
  • First and Second Laws of Thermodynamics

Unit 4: Electricity and Magnetism

Invisible fields that act at a distance and drive circuits.

  • Electric Charges, Fields and Gauss's Law
  • Electric Potential and Capacitance
  • Current Electricity and DC Circuits
  • Magnetic Fields and Forces
  • Electromagnetic Induction and AC Circuits

Unit 5: Optics and Modern Physics

Rays, waves and photons; the scale where energy comes in packets.

  • Ray Optics and Optical Instruments
  • Wave Optics
  • Modern Physics and Quantum Mechanics
